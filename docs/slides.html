The problem

Answers are buried in long documents

  • For example, 300 staff search a 40-page handbook, or just email HR
  • HR answers the same questions again and again
  • Wrong or inconsistent answers create real risk
Our solution

A tool that answers questions from your document, so staff get the right answer in seconds

  • Faster: for example, 300 staff × 10 min a week = about 50 hours a week back
  • Consistent: every answer comes from the same document
  • Trustworthy: the source passages are shown with each answer
What I built

Let me show you

  • Paste or upload a text document and save it
  • Ask in plain words, get an answer from that document
  • See the passages it used, and a clear reply when the answer isn't there
